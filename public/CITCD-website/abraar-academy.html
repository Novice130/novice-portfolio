About Abraar Academy

At Abraar Academy, we offer secular academics, Qur'anic education, and robust Islamic values instruction. Since its inception in 2012, we have been offering quality education with top-end amenities and a highly motivating environment.

We started with three students in 2012, and the hard work and dedication of our teaching staff, students, and a highly engaged parent body keeps us improving over time.
