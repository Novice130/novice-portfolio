📋 How It Works

  • Upload a Word (.docx, .doc) or PDF file containing questions
  • Each question is automatically placed on its own slide
  • Title text (if present) goes on a separate title slide
  • MCQ options are detected and formatted (works with inline or multi-line options)

✅ For Best Results

  • Number your questions (e.g., "1.", "Q1)", "(1)") for accurate splitting
  • Use standard option labels: A), B), C), D) or a., b., c., d.
  • Separate questions with blank lines if not numbered
  • Keep the title short (≤30 words) at the top of the document
