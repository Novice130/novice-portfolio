About the Cemetery

The American Muslim Cemetery is Illinois' first free Muslim cemetery, established to serve the Muslim community of Chicago's south suburbs with dignified burial services according to Islamic tradition.

We started providing burial washing services to indigent Muslim families free of charge and have grown to offer complete cemetery and funeral services.

✓ Free burial services for indigent families
✓ Ghusl (washing) facility on-site
✓ Islamic burial according to Sunnah
✓ 24/7 support for immediate needs

Muslim Funeral Facility

Complete funeral services according to Islamic tradition.

Ghusl Services

Proper Islamic washing (Ghusl) performed by trained staff in our dedicated facility, honoring the deceased with dignity and respect.

Kafan & Preparation

Complete shrouding (Kafan) services following the Sunnah, ensuring the deceased is prepared according to Islamic guidelines.

Janazah Prayer

Coordination of Janazah prayer services at our facility or local masjid, bringing the community together in remembrance.

Pre-Planning Services

Plan ahead to ease the burden on your family during difficult times.

Pre-planning your burial arrangements is a thoughtful way to ensure your wishes are honored while relieving your loved ones of difficult decisions during their time of grief.
